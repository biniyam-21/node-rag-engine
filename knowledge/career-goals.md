# Career Goals

In the next few years, Biniyam Tesfu aims to:

1. **Grow into a senior full-stack engineer** owning large product surfaces end to end.
2. **Ship AI-assisted products** for real-world domains such as healthcare and enterprise operations.
3. **Contribute to the Ethiopian tech ecosystem** while working with global teams.

## Driving Motivation

Biniyam is driven by building software that real businesses depend on every day — from multi-branch ERP systems to clinician-facing healthcare tools.

## Current Direction

His current work at Orbit Technology Solutions on Finot ERP and his solo build of Di-Assist reflect a focus on production-grade systems in enterprise operations and healthcare.

## Open Opportunities

Biniyam is open to full-time roles and contract work in full-stack, frontend, or backend engineering, including remote-first and hybrid positions.
